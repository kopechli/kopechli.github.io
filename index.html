My curious nature, from a young age was a drive, in my desire to understand how things work. I was interested in the "real world", all the things that were happening in the background. Why this, why that, and most of all, how. It didn't take long for Nature to fascinate me.

All this interconnected systems, the genius solutions, that we humans are trying to emulate. The information transmission in this natural systems particularly, was something that led an everlasting fire in me, that lead in subsequent years to my Bachelor in Information Management.

In 6th grade i was saving money from a zero allowance, to buy popular science, Focus or PC Magazine and around that time, my older brother got to go to computer school, I learned about operating systems and Pascal from his notes.

later on in high school i was introduced to algorithms, I excelled in computers and communication technology classes, and after that, it did not take me long to find a way to finance my first PC.

On that note, it was impossible for a curious mind not to "break" the PC, combine this with a limited finances and you have a situation ware you start to learn things, out of pure necessity and fast at that mater.

I dove into programing with C++ in my college years. During that time, i loved tweaking the source code and file structure of video games. Some of the subject i studied during that time was computer architecture, operating systems, computer networks computer algorithms, data communications, structural programing, data structures database systems, dystem analysis and Software engineering.

I put this studies to work later on in my internship and web developer roles. My intrests, studies and life itself, led me to join the army as cadet of Signals and Communication, from where i was discharged as second lieutenant with renewed passion about information technology.

I did some odd jobs since then, but i never abandoned my interests and my passion for information management and IT in general. My experience background is diverse and i may walk on different paths, but my direction is set, from long time ago.
